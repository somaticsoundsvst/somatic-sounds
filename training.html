Step into the world of sound & vibration

This three-day hybrid, online / in-person one on one training is designed to give you the knowledge, confidence, and hands-on experience to begin your journey as a sound healing practitioner.

Whether you're completely new to sound healing or looking to deepen your practice, this certification course will guide you into the art and science of working with Himalayan singing bowls — with clarity, confidence, and purpose.

You'll leave with a certification, practical skills, ongoing support, and — optionally — a personally selected set of high-quality Himalayan singing bowls for your practice.

Curriculum

What you'll explore

From the science to the sacred, with hands on practice.

1

Foundations of Sound Healing

What sound healing is, what people come to it seeking, and where it sits alongside other wellness and therapeutic practices.

2

History of the Bowls

Where these instruments come from, how they have been used across cultures and centuries, and the lineage you are stepping into.

3

Bowls, Mallets, and Care

Identifying bowls by shape, tone, and make. Choosing the right mallets, and keeping your instruments in good voice.

4

The Science of Sound

Vibration, frequency, resonance, and entrainment. How sound moves through the body, and what settles in the nervous system when it does.

5

Chakras and Mantras

The energetic body, the chakra system, and the Vedic roots of working with voice and tone as practice.

6

Playing the Bowls

Hands on time with technique. Rim play, striking, layering tones, and learning to listen while you play.

7
The heart of the training

Vibrational Sound Therapy (VST)

One on one work, and the deepest part of this course. You'll learn to place bowls around and directly on the body, drawing from the Somatic Resonance System alongside Himalayan and Vedic technique.

8

Introduction to Gongs

Gong types, basic technique, and how to bring a gong into a session without overwhelming the room.

9

Facilitating a Sound Bath

Structuring a session from open to close. Holding the room, reading the group, pacing, and knowing when to speak and when to stay quiet.

10

Building Your Practice

The practical side. Setting up your space, pricing your work, waivers and insurance, and finding your first clients.

11

Sonic Mastery and Ongoing Support

Tone arranged with purpose. Here you move from producing a clean tone to shaping it: different mallets, different textures, deliberate choices. Then where the work goes after certification, with continued mentorship and support as you find your own voice with sound.

This is the complete training. There is no Level I and Level II, no advanced follow-up you need to come back for.

Our Approach

A focused lineage

This training is dedicated entirely to Himalayan singing bowls. We don't cover crystal bowls, handpans, or other modalities — mastery, in our view, comes from going deep with one lineage of instruments rather than sampling across many.

Is This For You?

You're in the right place if...

You're called to share sound

You've experienced the power of sound healing and feel a pull to bring it to others — but don't know where to start.

You're a wellness professional

Yoga teachers, therapists, coaches, and bodyworkers looking to add a profound somatic tool to their existing practice.

You're on a personal journey

You don't need to become a practitioner — some people take this training purely to deepen their own healing and self-practice.
